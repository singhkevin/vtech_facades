03.2 Superior quality

Why VTech Facades builds with Fundermax.

Austria’s most resilient exterior panel, backed by our certified substructure engineering, in-house CNC milling, and precision installation.

ZERO FADING
01 // UV RESISTANCE Immune to Harsh Indian Sun

Patented double-hardened NT acrylic surface built for extreme tropical UV. Panels will never chalk, peel, crack, or fade—even after 15+ years under 45°C sun exposure.

100% WATERPROOF
02 // MONSOON RESISTANCE Zero Swelling, Rotting, or Termites

Non-porous, thermo-cured high-pressure compact core. Completely impervious to torrential monsoon downpours, coastal salinity, rot, and termite damage.

−30% INDOOR HEAT
03 // THERMAL EFFICIENCY Natural Chimney Cooling Envelope

Rear-ventilated cavity creates a continuous chimney airflow, flushing solar heat gain upward before it penetrates structural walls, drastically lowering HVAC loads.

10-YR GUARANTEE
04 // FACTORY ASSURANCE Direct Austrian Factory Guarantee

Certified EN 13501-1 Class B-s1, d0 fire retardancy, backed by an official 10-year Fundermax Austrian factory warranty and VTech certified installation.
