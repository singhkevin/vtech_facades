02 Solutions

Façade Solutions for Indian Buildings.

V-TECH offers customised façade solutions developed around the architecture, application, site conditions and desired visual character of each project.

01 Solution

Exterior HPL Cladding

Exterior-grade architectural panels for villas, apartments, commercial buildings and institutional projects. Available in coordinated colours, woodgrains, solids and material-inspired finishes.

02 Solution

Rear-Ventilated Façades

Cladding systems installed over a supporting framework with a ventilated cavity behind the exterior panel. Suitable for contemporary new-build and façade-renovation projects.

03 Solution

Architectural Glazing

Customised glass applications for elevations, entrances, reception areas, retail fronts and residential architecture.

04 Solution

Structural and Spider Glazing

Modern glazing solutions designed to create expansive transparent surfaces with clean, minimal architectural detailing.

05 Solution

Glass Canopies and Skylights

Overhead glass installations for building entrances, courtyards, terraces and naturally illuminated interior spaces.

06 Solution

CNC Façade Screens and Louvers

Decorative and functional façade elements developed for privacy, solar shading, ventilation and architectural identity.

07 Solution

Balcony and Railing Infill Panels

Panel applications for balconies and terraces that provide privacy, visual consistency and a coordinated exterior finish.

08 Solution

Interior Architectural HPL

Durable decorative panels for lobby walls, reception areas, feature surfaces, soffits and selected interior applications.
