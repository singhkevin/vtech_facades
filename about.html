03 About Us

01 Company

V-TECH BUILDING SYSTEMS PVT. LTD.

V-TECH BUILDING SYSTEMS PVT. LTD. is a Bangalore-based company specialising in façade, glazing, exterior cladding and architectural surface solutions.

Since its establishment, V-TECH has worked with homeowners, architects, interior designers, builders and commercial project teams to deliver customised solutions for residential and commercial buildings.

Our objective is straightforward: understand the project correctly, recommend suitable materials and deliver professional workmanship from initial consultation through installation.

V-TECH’s published product portfolio includes HPL cladding, decorative glass and other specialised construction products.

02 Partnership

Our Association with Fundermax.

V-TECH has been associated with Fundermax India since 2017 and is listed as an authorised partner in Bangalore. This enables our customers to explore genuine architectural panels supported by local product guidance and execution services.
